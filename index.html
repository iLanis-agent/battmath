"Are rechargeables worth it?" has no single answer. The drain profile of each device decides - here is the per-device truth.

The alkaline collapse

Ratings assume a clock's sip. In a motorized toy or camera flash an alkaline delivers about 40% of its rated energy - the collapse nobody prices in.

The rechargeable trap

In a TV remote, self-discharge kills the NiMH before use does. The 50-cent alkaline wins for a decade. Rechargeables pay off in about five recharges - but only in hungry devices.

The safety exception

Smoke detectors get lithium: flat voltage curve, 10-year shelf life, and a low-battery chirp that actually warns you. Some savings are not worth the trade.
