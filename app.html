$4/yr is a $20 charger over 5 years. The honest question is not "are rechargeables worth it" - it is per device, because drain profile decides everything.

The verdict

Why the honest answer is different per device

Alkaline ratings assume a clock's gentle sip. Put one in a motorized toy or camera flash and it delivers about 40% of its rated energy - that is the collapse nobody prices in. Rechargeables flip the math the other way in low-drain devices: a remote sips so slowly that self-discharge, not use, kills the NiMH, so the 50-cent alkaline wins for a decade. NiMH pays for itself in about five recharges in anything hungry. And safety devices are their own category: smoke detectors get lithium, because the flat voltage curve means the chirp warning actually works.
